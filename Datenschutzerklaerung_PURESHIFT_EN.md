## 2. General information on data processing

PURESHIFT is a shift planner app with employee management. All data you enter is stored **exclusively locally on your device**. The provider does not operate a server, does not receive any data and does not offer cloud synchronisation.

The App works completely offline. No registration or user account is required.

Data leaves your device only if you initiate this yourself (Section 5) or if your device's operating system performs a backup (Section 7).

## 3. Which data is processed locally?

The following data is stored on your device:

- Shifts and shift entries (date, times, notes), shift swap entries
- Shift types (name, colour, hours, surcharges) and shift groups
- Calendar and group names
- Settings for currency, salary and tax calculation, federal state, language, display and widgets
- Start time of a running time tracker (timer)
- PDF export templates and settings
- **Employee data**, if you use the employee management and fill it in (all fields are optional):
  - Name, date of birth
  - Contact details (private/work email, phone, mobile number, address)
  - Employment data (start/end date, type of employment, department, position, location)
  - Compensation data (salary, bank details, tax class)
  - Social security data (social security number, tax ID, health and pension insurance)
  - Vacation data as well as qualifications, certificates and key handover

For the Android widgets, a snapshot of your shifts is also stored locally on the device.

## 4. Responsibility for employee data

If you use the App to manage personal data of other people (e.g. employees), **you** are the data controller for that data under data protection law (e.g. as an employer). You must have a legal basis for the processing (e.g. Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR in conjunction with Section 26 of the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG), or the equivalent national law), inform the data subjects and protect the data appropriately (e.g. through a screen lock, device encryption and careful handling of export files).

The provider of the App has no access to this data and does not process it.

## 5. Data transfers initiated by the user

### 5.1 Backup / Restore

With the "Data backup" function you can export your App data (including employee data) as a JSON file and import it again. The file is **not encrypted** and is saved to the location you choose (device storage, cloud service, messenger, etc.). You are responsible for handling this file securely. The provider does not receive a copy.

### 5.2 PDF export

When a PDF document is created from your shift data, the file is generated locally. You can then share, print or save it. Nothing is transmitted to the provider.

### 5.3 Sharing a calendar

The "Share calendar" function creates an image of your shift calendar locally. You can send it to third parties using your device's share function. You alone decide with whom you share it.

### 5.4 Wi-Fi sharing (synchronisation between devices)

The App optionally offers "Wi-Fi sharing". If you activate it, the App starts a local server on your device that can be reached within the same local network (Wi-Fi). Another device (e.g. your second smartphone or a computer) can pair with it using a PIN shown by the App and then retrieve or send your App data. All App data, including employee data, is transferred.

Please note:

- Sharing is **switched off by default** and is only activated by you.
- Transfer takes place **within your local network** and **without encryption (HTTP)**. Use it only in trusted networks (not in public Wi-Fi).
- No data is sent to the provider or to servers on the internet.
- Paired devices can read data and, depending on the setting, merge or overwrite data without further confirmation. You can generate a new PIN at any time; all paired devices then lose access.
- Switch sharing off again after use.

## 6. Permissions

The App uses the following permissions and system functions:

- **Network access:** only for the optional Wi-Fi sharing (Section 5.4) and for opening this privacy policy in the browser.
- **Vibration:** for haptic feedback during use.
- **File access:** to save and read backup and PDF files. On Android, access is limited to folders you select yourself via the system picker.

The App does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts and does not send notifications. You can revoke granted permissions at any time in your device's system settings.

## 7. Backup by the operating system (Android)

Android can automatically back up app data to your Google account as part of the device backup (Google Backup). Whether and how this happens is controlled in the settings of your device or Google account. This is carried out by Google and not by the provider of the App; Google's privacy notices apply (https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you do not want App data to be backed up there, disable Google Backup on your device.

## 8. No analytics, tracking or advertising services

The App contains **no** analytics, tracking, advertising or social media services (e.g. no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no third-party crash reporting SDKs, no ad networks). **No device IDs** are collected for advertising or analytics purposes. For Wi-Fi sharing, the App locally generates a random device identifier that is used solely for pairing within your network and is not transmitted to the provider.

## 9. Processing by Google Play

When you download and install the App via the **Google Play Store**, Google processes personal data (e.g. Google account, email address, time of download, device ID). The provider of the App has no influence on this processing. Google's privacy notices apply: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 12. Rights of data subjects

Because the provider **does not collect or store personal data**, many data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection under Articles 15–21 GDPR) do not apply in relation to the provider. You can view, export, change or delete your locally stored data at any time yourself by uninstalling the App. Note that export files you have already created, copies on other devices (Wi-Fi sharing) and Google backups must be deleted separately.

People whose data you have recorded in the App contact you as the controller for their rights (Section 4).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a **data protection supervisory authority**.

## 13. Data security

Your data is stored in the App's internal storage on your device, which is protected by the operating system against access by other apps. The App does **not** additionally encrypt the data. You are responsible for the security of your device (e.g. screen lock, device encryption, regular updates). When using Wi-Fi sharing and when sharing export files, the notes in Section 5 apply.
