## 2. General information on data processing

PURESHIFT is a shift planner app with employee management. The shift and employee data you enter is stored locally on your Android device. For this app data, the provider does not operate a central server or its own cloud synchronisation. In normal operation, the provider does not receive a copy of this data.

The core functions of the App can be used offline. The optional Wi-Fi sharing requires a local network. Internet access is required if you open the legal information linked in the App on GitHub.

Data can leave your device if you export or share it yourself (Section 5), if Android performs a device backup (Section 7) or if you open external links (Section 10). If you pass data on to another app or a cloud service, their privacy terms also apply.

No registration or user account is required.

## 3. Which data is processed locally?

The following data is stored on your device:

- Shifts and shift entries (date, times, notes and, where applicable, a work location you have entered) as well as shift swap entries
- Shift types (name, colour, hours and breaks) and shift groups
- Calendar and group names
- Settings for currency, salary and tax calculation, federal state, language, display and widgets
- Start time of a running time tracker (timer)
- PDF export templates and settings
- **Employee data**, if you use the employee management and fill it in (all fields are optional):
  - Name and date of birth
  - Contact details (private/work email, phone, mobile number and address)
  - Employment data (start/end date, type of employment, department, position and location)
  - Compensation data (salary, bank details and tax class)
  - Social security data (social security number, tax ID, health and pension insurance)
  - Vacation data as well as qualifications, trainings, certificates and key handover

For Android widgets, a local summary of upcoming shifts is also stored.

For Wi-Fi sharing, the App stores technical synchronisation data, including the pairing PIN, identifiers and access tokens of paired devices, the time of the last synchronisation and the setting for automatically accepting write operations.

## 4. Responsibility for employee data

If you use the App to manage personal data of other people, for example employees, you must check who is responsible for this processing under data protection law. Depending on the use, this may in particular be your employer or your company.

The responsible party must have a legal basis for the processing, inform the data subjects and protect the data appropriately. Special legal requirements may apply to employee data.

In normal operation, the provider has no access to the locally stored employee data. Backups, transfers initiated by you and external services are described in the following sections.

## 5. Data transfers initiated by the user

### 5.1 Backup / Restore

With the "Data backup" function you can export App data as a JSON file and import it again. A full export may also contain employee data.

With a period export, only shift entries and shift swap entries are limited to the selected period. Other data, such as employee master data and settings, may still be included in full.

The JSON file is not encrypted. On Android, you choose the storage location through the system file picker. If you select a cloud, messenger or other third-party app for this, its provider may receive the file and process it under its own privacy terms. The provider of PURESHIFT does not receive a copy.

### 5.2 PDF export

When a PDF document is created from your shift data, the file is generated locally on the device. You can then share, print or save it using system functions. If you select another app or an external service for this, it processes the file under its own privacy terms. The PDF export does not transmit anything to the provider of PURESHIFT.

### 5.3 Sharing a calendar

The "Share calendar" function creates an image of your shift calendar locally. You can then send this image to other people or apps using your device's share function. You decide yourself with whom you share it.

### 5.4 Wi-Fi sharing (synchronisation between devices)

The App optionally offers "Wi-Fi sharing". If you activate it, the App starts a local server on your device that can be reached in the same local network. Another device can pair with it using the PIN shown and then retrieve or send App data. All App data, including employee data, can be transferred in the process.

Please note:

- Wi-Fi sharing is switched off by default and is only activated by you.
- Transfer takes place within the local network over HTTP and is not encrypted. Use the function only in trusted networks, not in public Wi-Fi.
- No central server of the provider is used for synchronisation.
- Paired devices can read data.
- By default, write operations from paired devices are accepted without further confirmation ("Always accept", shown in the App as "Immer akzeptieren"). If you deactivate this setting, incoming write operations are rejected; no confirmation prompt is shown.
- If you generate a new PIN, existing pairings are revoked.
- Switch Wi-Fi sharing off again after use.

## 6. Permissions and system functions

The App uses network and system functions for the following purposes:

- **Network:** for the optional Wi-Fi sharing, for displaying the local network address and for opening the linked privacy policy and legal notice in the browser
- **Vibration:** for feedback during use
- **File and sharing functions:** for importing and exporting backup files and for sharing PDF and calendar files. On Android, you select files or storage locations through the system picker.

The App does not use the camera, microphone, GPS location or contacts. A work location or employee location that you enter manually is not a GPS location. The App does not send push notifications.

## 7. Backup by the operating system (Android)

The Android App is permitted for device backups. If backup is enabled on your device or in your Google account, App data can be automatically included in a Google backup. This may also include locally stored shift and employee data.

Whether a backup is made depends on Android and your device and account settings. The backup is carried out by Google and not by the provider of PURESHIFT. Google's privacy notices apply: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you do not want such a backup, check and change the backup settings of your device and Google account.

## 8. No analytics, tracking or advertising services

The App contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or social media services. No device identifiers are collected for advertising or analytics purposes.

For Wi-Fi sharing, the App uses a local device identifier and access tokens for paired devices. This data serves synchronisation in the local network and is not transmitted to the provider.

## 9. Processing by Google Play

When you download or update the App via Google Play, Google processes data in connection with operating the store and providing the App. Which data is processed depends, among other things, on your Google account, your device and your settings.

The provider of PURESHIFT has no influence on this processing. Google's privacy notices apply: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 12. Rights of data subjects

The shift and employee data entered in the App is stored locally on your device and is not available to the provider through a central server. The provider therefore cannot retrieve, change or delete this local content from a central database.

This does not mean that rights against parties that actually process data lapse. For data you pass on through backups, external links or third-party apps, the parties involved and their privacy information are decisive. People whose data you record in the App contact the party responsible for this processing for their rights, for example their employer.

You can view, change and export your local data in the App. Uninstalling the App generally removes the locally stored App data from the device. Export files, copies on other devices and Android backups may have to be deleted separately.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

## 13. Data security

The App stores its data in the device's internal app storage. This is shielded by the operating system from access by other apps. The App does not additionally encrypt the stored data.

You are responsible for protecting your device. This includes in particular a device lock, device encryption and up-to-date software. When using Wi-Fi sharing and when sharing files, the notes in Section 5 also apply.
